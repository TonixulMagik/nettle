Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 August 2026

Roost is in active development. As features are added or removed, what Roost stores changes with them. This page is updated whenever that happens, and the date above always tells you when it was last true.

Roost is a Discord application. This page explains exactly what data it stores, why it stores it, and how you can have it deleted.

Not every feature runs on every server. Section 2 lists what Roost stores wherever it is added. Section 3 lists extra data that exists only on servers where the community and economy features are switched on, which today is a small number of servers the developer runs directly. If those features are not enabled where you are, none of the data in section 3 exists for you.

We collect as little as possible. Everything below exists because a feature cannot work without it.

1. Who is responsible

Roost is operated by an individual developer in Romania, not a company. For anything in this document, including data deletion requests, contact tonixbusiness.ro@gmail.com or use our support server.

2. What we store everywhere

Identifiers

  • Your Discord user ID — a number, not your name or email.
  • Server (guild) IDs of servers Roost has been added to.
  • Channel and role IDs that a server administrator configures, so Roost knows where to post and who counts as staff.

Support tickets

  • Who opened the ticket, which category, and when.
  • The messages sent inside that ticket channel, saved as a transcript when the ticket is closed, so the server's staff keep a record of what was discussed.
  • Files attached inside the ticket, re-uploaded alongside the transcript.
  • Whether a user has been blocked from opening further tickets on that server, and why.

Roles, giveaways and polls

  • Which self-assignable roles you selected.
  • Which giveaways you entered and which you won, so entries cannot be duplicated and a winner can be re-rolled.
  • Which option you voted for in a poll, so the same person cannot vote twice.

Timestamps are stored alongside all of the above.

3. Extra data, only where community features are enabled

On servers running Roost's economy and community features, the following also exists. All of it is per server: it is a game score, not a record about you as a person.

Game economy

  • Your in-game balance, bank balance and safe, none of which have or can be exchanged for real-world value.
  • Your in-game job, work streak and number of shifts.
  • Items you own, shop purchases, subscriptions and businesses.
  • Bets placed and results in the in-game games, and lottery tickets.

Activity counters

  • How many messages you sent and how many minutes you spent in voice, used for levels and leaderboards.
  • Which channels you were active in, as counts.

This stores numbers, not text. Roost checks that a message is long enough to count and then increments a counter. The message itself is never saved. See section 4.

Moderation records

  • Warnings, timeouts, bans and other sanctions issued to you on that server, with the reason, who issued it and when.
  • Whether you are on that server's blacklist.
  • If you are a staff member: your position and its change history.

Optional, only if you provide it

  • Your birthday, day and month only. Never the year, so your age is not stored or inferable. You choose to give this; you can remove it at any time.
  • Who invited you to the server and when you joined, used for invite counts.

4. Message content, stated plainly

Roost uses the MESSAGE_CONTENT privileged intent. It is used for exactly two things, and both are listed here:

  1. Ticket transcripts. When a ticket is closed, the messages inside that ticket channel are saved into a transcript for the server's staff. This is the only case where message text is stored.
  2. Measuring message length for activity counting, on servers where that feature is enabled. Roost checks whether a message is long enough to count towards your level, then adds one to a counter. The text is read, evaluated and discarded. It is never written anywhere.

Beyond those two:

  • Roost does not log or store messages from normal channels.
  • Roost does not read or store direct messages.
  • Roost does not analyse, profile or train anything on message content.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or trade your data. Not to anyone, ever.
  • We do not use your data for advertising or profiling.
  • We do not combine your data across unrelated servers to build a profile of you.
  • We do not store your email address, phone number, IP address or payment details, because Roost never asks for them.
  • We do not use third-party analytics inside the bot.
  • Nothing in Roost can be bought with real money, and nothing in it can be exchanged for real money.

6. Who can see your data

Ticket transcripts are posted to a channel chosen by that server's administrators and are visible to that server's staff. That is the point of a transcript, and it is worth knowing before you write something in a ticket.

Moderation records are visible to that server's staff. Leaderboards and profiles are visible to members of the server they belong to.

Nobody outside a server receives that server's data. The developer can access the database for maintenance and debugging, and for nothing else.

7. How long we keep it

  • Configuration data is kept while Roost is on the server.
  • Ticket transcripts are kept until deleted by that server's staff or by request.
  • Giveaway, poll, economy and moderation records are kept while Roost is on the server, so results and histories stay verifiable.
  • If Roost is removed from a server, that server's data is marked inactive and deleted after 90 days, unless Roost is added back before then.
  • Your birthday is deleted as soon as you ask, with no waiting period.

8. Your rights and how to use them

You can ask us, at any time, to:

  • tell you what data we hold about you;
  • send you a copy of it;
  • correct it;
  • delete it.

Write to tonixbusiness.ro@gmail.com from any address, or open a ticket on our support server, and include your Discord user ID. We will answer within 30 days.

Two limits worth stating honestly:

  • If you ask us to delete a ticket transcript, we can remove it from our database, but a copy may already sit in a message posted on the server where the ticket was opened. Only that server's administrators can delete that copy. We will tell you when this is the case.
  • A server's staff may keep their own moderation records of sanctions issued to you, as servers are entitled to do. Deleting your data from Roost does not erase a server's own record of a decision it made.

9. Children

Discord requires users to be at least 13 years old, or older where local law demands it. Roost is not intended for anyone below that age. If you believe a child's data has been stored, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Where data is stored

Data is stored on servers located in the European Union. Roost is operated from Romania and is subject to Romanian and European Union law, including the GDPR.

11. Changes to this policy

Roost is being built, so this policy is not finished either. Every time a new feature stores something that is not listed above, this page is updated before or at the same time as that feature goes live.

When it changes, the date at the top changes with it, and anything significant, in particular any new kind of data being collected, is announced on our support server. Continuing to use Roost after a change means you accept the updated policy. If a change is one you are not happy with, remove Roost from your server and ask us to delete your data.
