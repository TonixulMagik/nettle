Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026

Nettle is in active development. As features are added or removed, what Nettle stores changes with them. This page is updated whenever that happens, and the date above always tells you when it was last true.

Nettle is a Discord application. This page explains exactly what data it stores, why it stores it, and how you can have it deleted.

Not every feature runs on every server. Section 2 lists what Nettle stores wherever it is added. Section 3 lists extra data that exists only on servers where the community and economy features are switched on, which today is a small number of servers the developer runs directly. If those features are not enabled where you are, none of the data in section 3 exists for you.

We collect as little as possible. Everything below exists because a feature cannot work without it.

1. Who is responsible

Nettle is operated by an individual developer in Romania, not a company. For anything in this document, including data deletion requests, contact tonixbusiness.ro@gmail.com or use our support server.

2. What we store everywhere

Identifiers

  • Your Discord user ID — a number, not your name or email.
  • Server (guild) IDs of servers Nettle has been added to.
  • Channel and role IDs that a server administrator configures, so Nettle knows where to post and who counts as staff.

Support tickets

  • Who opened the ticket, which category, and when.
  • The messages sent inside that ticket channel, saved as a transcript when the ticket is closed, so the server's staff keep a record of what was discussed.
  • Files attached inside the ticket, re-uploaded alongside the transcript.
  • Whether a user has been blocked from opening further tickets on that server, and why.

Roles, giveaways and polls

  • Which self-assignable roles you selected.
  • Which giveaways you entered and which you won, so entries cannot be duplicated and a winner can be re-rolled.
  • Which option you voted for in a poll, so the same person cannot vote twice.

Timestamps are stored alongside all of the above.

3. Extra data, only where community features are enabled

On servers running Nettle's economy and community features, the following also exists. All of it is per server: it is a game score, not a record about you as a person.

Game economy

  • Your in-game balance, bank balance and safe, none of which have or can be exchanged for real-world value.
  • Your in-game job, work streak and number of shifts.
  • Items you own, shop purchases, subscriptions and businesses.
  • Bets placed and results in the in-game games, and lottery tickets.

Activity counters

  • How many messages you sent and how many minutes you spent in voice, used for levels and leaderboards.
  • Which channels you were active in, as counts.

This stores numbers, not text. Nettle checks that a message is long enough to count and then increments a counter. The message itself is never saved. See section 4.

Moderation records

  • Warnings, timeouts, bans and other sanctions issued to you on that server, with the reason, who issued it and when.
  • Whether you are on that server's blacklist.
  • If you are a staff member: your position and its change history.

Forms you choose to fill in

Some servers run an application form for staff positions or for a bot-testing programme. These are entirely optional. If you submit one, we store the answers exactly as you typed them, together with who reviewed it, the decision and the reason. Two things to know before you fill one in:

  • The staff application asks how old you are. If you answer, that answer is stored as part of the form. Do not fill in the form if you would rather not tell us.
  • Bug reports in the testing programme store the text you write describing the problem.

Form answers are visible to that server's staff who review applications. You can ask for yours to be deleted at any time, as described in section 8.

Optional, only if you provide it

  • Your birthday, day and month only. Never the year, so your age cannot be worked out from it. You choose to give this; you can remove it at any time.
  • Who invited you to the server and when you joined, used for invite counts.

4. Message content, stated plainly

Nettle uses the MESSAGE_CONTENT privileged intent. It is used for exactly three things, and all three are listed here:

  1. Ticket transcripts. When a ticket is closed, the messages inside that ticket channel are saved into a transcript for the server's staff. This is the only case where message text is stored.
  2. Measuring message length for activity counting, on servers where that feature is enabled. Nettle checks whether a message is long enough to count towards your level, then adds one to a counter. The text is read, evaluated and discarded. It is never written anywhere.
  3. Replying when you mention the bot, on the developer's own server only. If you write a message that mentions Nettle directly, the text of that message and the last few messages of that channel are sent to Google's Gemini API to generate a reply, which is then posted in the channel. This happens only when you mention the bot on purpose, only on the single server the developer runs directly, and never on any other server. Nothing sent this way is stored by Nettle. Google processes it under their own terms. If this feature is not enabled where you are, none of your messages are ever sent anywhere.

Beyond those three:

  • Nettle does not log or store messages from normal channels.
  • Nettle does not read direct messages, with one exception: the developer's own DMs with the bot, read only so the developer can give it commands. Nobody else's DMs are read, and none are stored.
  • Nettle does not profile you, and does not train any model on your messages.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or trade your data. Not to anyone, ever.
  • We do not use your data for advertising or profiling.
  • We do not combine your data across unrelated servers to build a profile of you.
  • We do not store your email address, phone number, IP address or payment details, because Nettle never asks for them.
  • We do not use third-party analytics inside the bot.
  • Nothing in Nettle can be bought with real money, and nothing in it can be exchanged for real money.

6. Who can see your data

Ticket transcripts are posted to a channel chosen by that server's administrators and are visible to that server's staff. That is the point of a transcript, and it is worth knowing before you write something in a ticket.

One outside service is involved, and only for the feature described in section 4, point 3: Google receives the text of messages that mention the bot, on the developer's own server, in order to generate the reply. It receives nothing else, from no other server. No other third party receives your data.

Moderation records are visible to that server's staff. Leaderboards and profiles are visible to members of the server they belong to.

Nobody outside a server receives that server's data. The developer can access the database for maintenance and debugging, and for nothing else.

7. How long we keep it

  • Configuration data is kept while Nettle is on the server.
  • Ticket transcripts are kept until deleted by that server's staff or by request.
  • Giveaway, poll, economy and moderation records are kept while Nettle is on the server, so results and histories stay verifiable.
  • If Nettle is removed from a server, that server's data stops being used, and we delete it on request from anyone who administrated that server. Automatic deletion on a fixed schedule is not in place yet; when it is, this page will say so and give the period.
  • Your birthday is deleted when you ask, and we treat it as a priority over the 30 day window in section 8.

8. Your rights and how to use them

You can ask us, at any time, to:

  • tell you what data we hold about you;
  • send you a copy of it;
  • correct it;
  • delete it.

Write to tonixbusiness.ro@gmail.com from any address, or open a ticket on our support server, and include your Discord user ID. We will answer within 30 days.

Two limits worth stating honestly:

  • If you ask us to delete a ticket transcript, we can remove it from our database, but a copy may already sit in a message posted on the server where the ticket was opened. Only that server's administrators can delete that copy. We will tell you when this is the case.
  • A server's staff may keep their own moderation records of sanctions issued to you, as servers are entitled to do. Deleting your data from Nettle does not erase a server's own record of a decision it made.

9. Children

Discord requires users to be at least 13 years old, or older where local law demands it. Nettle is not intended for anyone below that age. If you believe a child's data has been stored, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Where data is stored

Data is stored on servers located in the European Union. Nettle is operated from Romania and is subject to Romanian and European Union law, including the GDPR.

11. Changes to this policy

Nettle is being built, so this policy is not finished either. Every time a new feature stores something that is not listed above, this page is updated before or at the same time as that feature goes live.

When it changes, the date at the top changes with it, and anything significant, in particular any new kind of data being collected, is announced on our support server. Continuing to use Nettle after a change means you accept the updated policy. If a change is one you are not happy with, remove Nettle from your server and ask us to delete your data.
