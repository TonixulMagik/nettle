Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 August 2026

Roost is in active development. As features are added or removed, what Roost stores will change with them. This page is updated whenever that happens, and the date above always tells you when it was last true.

Roost is a Discord application that provides support tickets, self-assignable roles, giveaways and polls. This page explains exactly what data Roost stores, why it stores it, and how you can have it deleted.

We collect as little as possible. Everything listed below exists because a feature cannot work without it.

1. Who is responsible

Roost is operated by an individual developer, not a company. For anything in this document, including data deletion requests, contact us at tonixbusiness.ro@gmail.com or through our support server.

2. What we store

Identifiers

  • Your Discord user ID — a number, not your name or email. Stored when you open a ticket, claim a role, enter a giveaway, or vote in a poll.
  • Server (guild) IDs of servers Roost has been added to.
  • Channel and role IDs that a server administrator configures during setup, so Roost knows where to post and who counts as staff.

Support tickets

  • Who opened the ticket, which category it was opened in, and when.
  • The messages sent inside that ticket channel, saved as a transcript when the ticket is closed, so the server's staff keep a record of what was discussed.
  • Files attached inside the ticket, re-uploaded alongside the transcript.
  • Whether a user has been blocked from opening further tickets on that server, and why.

Giveaways and polls

  • Which users entered a giveaway, and which users won, so entries cannot be duplicated and a winner can be re-rolled.
  • Which option a user voted for in a poll, so the same person cannot vote twice.

Roles

  • Which self-assignable roles you selected, so the menu can show your current choice.

Timestamps are stored alongside all of the above.

3. Message content, stated plainly

Roost uses the MESSAGE_CONTENT privileged intent. It is used for exactly one purpose: building the transcript of a support ticket.

Concretely, this means:

  • Roost reads messages inside ticket channels only, and only when a ticket is closed.
  • Roost does not read, log, or store messages from your server's normal channels.
  • Roost does not read or store direct messages.
  • Roost does not analyse message content for any purpose other than writing it into the transcript that the server's own staff receive.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or trade your data. Not to anyone, ever.
  • We do not use your data for advertising or profiling.
  • We do not track you across servers.
  • We do not store your email address, phone number, IP address or payment details, because Roost never asks for them.
  • We do not use third-party analytics inside the bot.

5. Who can see your data

Ticket transcripts are posted to a channel chosen by the administrators of the server the ticket was opened on, and are visible to that server's staff. That is the point of a transcript, and it is worth knowing before you write something in a ticket.

Nobody outside that server receives your data. The developer can access the database for maintenance and debugging, and for nothing else.

6. How long we keep it

  • Configuration data is kept while Roost is on the server.
  • Ticket transcripts are kept until deleted by the server's staff or by request.
  • Giveaway and poll records are kept so past results stay verifiable.
  • If Roost is removed from a server, that server's data is marked inactive and deleted after 90 days, unless it is restored by adding Roost back before then.

7. Your rights and how to use them

You can ask us, at any time, to:

  • tell you what data we hold about you;
  • send you a copy of it;
  • correct it;
  • delete it.

Write to tonixbusiness.ro@gmail.com from any address, or open a ticket on our support server, and include your Discord user ID. We will answer within 30 days.

One limit worth stating honestly: if you ask us to delete a ticket transcript, we can remove it from our database, but a copy may already sit in a message posted on the server where the ticket was opened. Only that server's administrators can delete that copy. We will tell you when this is the case.

8. Children

Discord requires users to be at least 13 years old, or older where local law demands it. Roost is not intended for anyone below that age. If you believe a child's data has been stored, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Where data is stored

Data is stored on servers located in the European Union. Roost is operated from Romania and is subject to Romanian and European Union law, including the GDPR.

10. Changes to this policy

Roost is being built, so this policy is not finished either. Every time a new feature stores something that is not listed above, this page is updated before or at the same time as that feature goes live.

When it changes, the date at the top changes with it, and anything significant, in particular any new kind of data being collected, is announced on our support server. Continuing to use Roost after a change means you accept the updated policy. If a change is one you are not happy with, remove Roost from your server and ask us to delete your data.

11. Contact

Email tonixbusiness.ro@gmail.com, or join our support server. We read everything.
